North Echo Security Research is the independent research practice of Christopher Lusk. Recent work covers industrial control systems (Siemens SICAM, Inductive Automation Ignition, OpENer), Kubernetes and Linux platforms, and AI agent frameworks and the supply chain around them.

Every finding is reproduced on lab infrastructure before it is reported, and disclosed in coordination with the vendor. The full method is on the [Standards](/standards/) page, and published advisories are listed under [Disclosures](/disclosures/).
